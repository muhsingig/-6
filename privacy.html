privacy policy

last modified: 11/09/2026

introduction

This policy explains what personal information Stay Purr (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit our website or book cat sitting, how we use it and the choices you have. We only collect what we need to look after your cat and run our business.

information we collect

Contact details you give us (name, email, phone, home address); details about your cat (name, age, health, medication, routine and photos); home-access details such as key arrangements or door codes; booking and payment records; and basic, anonymous analytics about how the website is used.

how we use it

To arrange and carry out visits, send you updates and photos, contact you or your emergency contact, arrange veterinary care if needed, take payment, and improve our service. We do not sell your information and never use your cat’s photos publicly without your permission.

keys and home access

Keys are coded so they cannot be linked to your address if lost, and door codes are stored encrypted and shared only with the sitters assigned to you. Access details are deleted or keys returned when you stop using the service.

sharing

We share information only with the sitters caring for your cat, your chosen vet in an emergency, and service providers who help us run the business (for example payment and messaging tools), under agreements that require them to protect it.

your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@staypurr.com. We keep booking records only as long as needed for legal and accounting purposes.

contact

Questions about this policy? Write to hello@staypurr.com and we’ll reply within five working days.
